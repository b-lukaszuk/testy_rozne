* Getting Started

type `:?` at GHCI to list help options/hints.

An arithmetic quirk: writing negative numbers:

#+begin_src

-- works OK
-3

-- but this one produces an error,
-- since unary, - operator, cannot be mixed with infix operators
2 + -3

-- use parenthesis instead
2 + (-3)

-- or negate function
2 + negate 3

#+end_src

Other quirks:

#+begin_src

-- \= instead of !=
2 \= 3

-- not instead of !
not True

#+end_src

Info on operators (associativity, precedence, etc.)

#+begin_src

-- :i short for :info
:i +

-- or

:i (*)

#+end_src

Mathematical constants:

#+begin_src

-- famous pi
pi

-- but no famous e
e -- produces error

-- for e use
exp 1

#+end_src

Exponentiation operators:

#+begin_src

-- ^ to raise to integer power
2^10

-- ** to raise to float power
9 ** (0.5) -- equivalent to: sqrt 9

#+end_src
